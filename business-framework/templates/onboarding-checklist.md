# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

**Employee:** ____________  **Role:** ____________  **Start date:** ____________
**Manager:** ____________  **Buddy:** ____________  **Probation ends:** ____________

## Pre-boarding (before Day 1)
- [ ] Offer letter signed and returned
- [ ] Documents collected: ID and address proof, PAN, Aadhaar (with consent), education and experience certificates, relieving letter, last 3 payslips, bank details, photo, emergency contact, UAN (if any)
- [ ] Background and reference checks complete
- [ ] Email, software logins and access cards created
- [ ] Laptop, desk, ID card, visiting cards and uniform ready
- [ ] Buddy assigned (a peer, not the manager)
- [ ] Welcome email sent (time, place, dress code, Day 1 agenda)
- [ ] Team told who is joining and why

## Day 1: Welcome
- [ ] Founder or manager welcome: company story, mission, values
- [ ] Office tour and introductions
- [ ] Appointment letter issued; policies acknowledged
- [ ] PF/ESI nomination forms filled; insurance enrolment
- [ ] All tools set up and logins tested
- [ ] 30/60/90 plan walked through
- [ ] Team lunch
- [ ] End-of-day check-in: "What questions do you have?"

## Week 1: Learn
- [ ] Training on products, services and the top 5 customer problems
- [ ] SOPs for the role read
- [ ] Shadowed a senior colleague for 2–3 days
- [ ] One small, real deliverable completed
- [ ] Met key cross-functional people
- [ ] Week-1 feedback conversation with the manager

## 30 / 60 / 90: Ramp
- [ ] **Day 30:** knows the products, tools and processes; owns small tasks independently
- [ ] **Day 60:** owns a full workflow; contributes ideas; KPIs agreed
- [ ] **Day 90:** fully productive against KPIs; probation review; confirmation decision
- [ ] Onboarding survey completed: what should we improve?
