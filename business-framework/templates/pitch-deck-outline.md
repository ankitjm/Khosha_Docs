# 12-Slide Pitch Deck Outline

1. **Title:** company name, one-line description, logo, contact.
2. **Problem:** the painful, expensive problem. One real customer story and one data point.
3. **Solution:** your product and why it is 10× better.
4. **Why now:** the trend, regulation or technology shift that makes this the moment.
5. **Market size:** TAM / SAM / SOM, built bottom-up (number of customers × price).
6. **Product:** screenshots or a demo, the key features, the roadmap.
7. **Traction:** revenue, growth rate, customers, retention, logos, testimonials.
8. **Business model:** pricing, unit economics (CAC, LTV, gross margin).
9. **Go-to-market:** channels, sales motion, partnerships.
10. **Competition:** landscape and your unfair advantage.
11. **Team:** why this team wins; advisors; key hires planned.
12. **The ask:** amount, instrument, use of funds, milestones it will reach.

**Also prepare:** a one-page teaser, a 3-year monthly financial model, and a data room (see Chapter 05 of the e-book).
